For this assignment, we took code from the Internet and programmed it so that a switch would turn on the buzzer and play the song that was in the code. This tested our ability to use conditionals such as if/else. I chose Happy Birthday. This was also our first time using the buzzer, nearly completing our knowledge of all the LilyPad can do. 

A note for this assignment: Despite it being in the loop, my code wouldn't play over and over for some reason, even though it does work. The delay is intense, and I will discuss in office or TA hours to get more clarity on this. 

A tip I would've given past me is actually try to write a simple song myself. I know music theory and I actually found it more difficult to use the Internet code because I was parsing through syntax I didn't understand. While writing my own would've been a lot more tedious and definitely less concise, it would've made for tracing code easier and I likely wouldn't have ran into my looping issue.
