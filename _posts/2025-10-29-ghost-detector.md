For this assignment, we combined our knowledge of Arduino to activate the light and temperature sensors. We had to create a code where the red lights would only turn on if it was cold and dark. 

A tip I would have told my past self would be to test a lot of different temperature values because oftentimes the sensor was really sensitive and just required a lot of trial and error. Also, check for spelling errors in your code!
