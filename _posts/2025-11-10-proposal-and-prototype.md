For my final project, I am proposing a mug sleeve that, once the button is pressed, will activate the temperature sensor. If the temperature sensor senses a particular high or low temperature (indicating a ready-to-drink hot or iced drink), it will play a certain tune and light up a specific color to indicate that the drink is at its optimal temperature.

I'm excited about this project because I'm an avid cafe lover, tea fanatic, and work as a Goodrich barista on campus, so mugs weirdly are pretty big part of my everyday life. I'm excited to combine my coursework with my hobbies! 

My paper prototype details the different supplies that will be essential to the project, though I still have some questions about how I can optimize the sewing of the different components.
