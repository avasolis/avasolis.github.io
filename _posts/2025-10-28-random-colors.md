For this assignment, we continued to develop our knowledge of what we can do using RGB and Arduino to make the RGB colors flash at random times. 

A tip I would have told my past self is to make sure the integers are in the correct order -- I know it's trivial and something we've been working on for a while, but it really makes a big difference in catching careless mistakes when checking your code.
