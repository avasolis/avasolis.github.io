For this assignment, we created our own void functions independent of void setup and loop to have our buzzer play either SOS or our names in morse code depending on whether or not the circuit was closed. 

A tip I would give to my past self is to review the worksheet from class in depth before trying to create my own void functions. The code that worked is actually not dissimilar from the worksheet and that would have saved me a lot of time!
