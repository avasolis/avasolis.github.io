For this assignment, I used my three state-based approaches to the flashbulb, big dipper, and synchronous assignments to put together a larger piece of code all under one loop. This assignment continued to build our knowledge of Arduino as well as the LilyPad. 

A tip I would have told my past self is to really trace each part of the code and check it in small chunks when putting together a larger piece of code. My code was sporatically working and I had difficulty tracing where the issue was. Another helpful tip is to start early and schedule office hours into my day.
