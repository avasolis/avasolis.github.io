Similar to our random colors assignment, the analogs arrays assignment has random PWM pins on the Arduino light up using arrays, a continuation of our knowledge of for loops and void functions. 

A tip I would give my past self is to study the POGIL in-class worksheets thoroughly before attempting the assignment -- they helped immensely, and it's much harder to figure it out simply from scratch.
